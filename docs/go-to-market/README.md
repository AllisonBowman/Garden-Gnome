# Go-to-market for PlantAdvocate 1.1.3

This folder holds the plan for launching PlantAdvocate on the App Store and the eight months after it, written in October 2026 against the ship tip `3f6d7da`. Everything here is a draft. Nothing in it has been sent, posted, filed or submitted, and every outward step is Allison's to take, change or skip.

Start with [strategy.md](strategy.md), which opens with the plan in brief: what to do this week, at launch, through the winter and in spring, and why.

[product-truth.md](product-truth.md) is the product truth sheet: what 1.1.3 does and doesn't do, the numbers that may be quoted, and the exact wording to use. Every claim in every other file is checked against it, and where they disagree it wins.

[strategy.md](strategy.md) is the go-to-market strategy: positioning, audiences, the fixes the marketing depends on, the product page and ratings plan, the waitlist, channels and their rules, measurement without trackers, the timeline to June 2027 with Allison's weekly time, budget, risks and the decisions only Allison can make.

[research/](research/) holds the evidence the strategy rests on. [competitors.md](research/competitors.md) covers the plant-care, planner and pet-toxicity apps. [channels.md](research/channels.md) covers where the audiences gather and each community's promotion rules. [aso.md](research/aso.md) covers the App Store listing and Apple's launch mechanics. [review-risk.md](research/review-risk.md) covers the chance and causes of an App Review rejection. [launch-playbook.md](research/launch-playbook.md) covers what zero-budget indie launches show and the legal and trademark guardrails.

[launch-kit/](launch-kit/) holds the ready-to-edit drafts that the strategy calls for, such as the waitlist email, review replies, promotional text and any outreach notes, each checked against the truth sheet.

[targets.md](targets.md) lists the specific outlets, communities and programs worth contacting, with each one's own public contact or submission page and the rule that governs it.

[launch-day-runbook.md](launch-day-runbook.md) is the step-by-step checklist for release day and the week after it, with the pre-launch checklist and the check-ins for the first 30 days and the winter.

[launch-day-site.md](launch-day-site.md) sets out the edits to `site/` that go live once the App Store page loads: the store links, the Smart App Banner, Apple's credit line, the press page, the Open Graph tags and the checks that prove them, with the Apple rules each follows. They wait on branch `claude/launch-day-site`, reviewed on 2026-10-31. Nothing on it goes out before launch day, and the campaign-label privacy clause follows on L+1 or L+2 with the `site` token.
